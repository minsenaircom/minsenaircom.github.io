What is the minimum order quantity?
Standard MOQ starts at 500 units for first-time OEM partners. Repeat orders can be as low as 50-100 units depending on the product line.

Can I customize the appearance and branding?
Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM customization including housing color, logo branding, control panel layout, and custom packaging with your brand design.

What certifications are available for BKJ-300A?
Certification availability varies by model, configuration and destination market. Applicable requirements, test scope and documentation are confirmed during project review.

What is the typical lead time?
Sample production: 7-15 days. Mass production: 25-40 days depending on order quantity and customization level.
