Our Production Facility

Located in Zhongshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's appliance manufacturing hub. Our 8,000m² facility houses multiple dedicated assembly lines, injection molding equipment, an in-house testing laboratory, and a dedicated R&D team of 12 engineers.

We operate 7 specialized production lines covering the full range of home environment appliances — dehumidifiers, air purifiers, ice makers, space heaters, humidifiers, cooling fans, and air conditioners. Each line is staffed by experienced technicians and follows strict quality control procedures.

R&D Capabilities

Our R&D team specializes in air treatment technology including HEPA filtration systems, activated carbon adsorption, compressor-based dehumidification, PTC ceramic heating, ultrasonic humidification, and evaporative cooling. We also develop smart IoT controls with WiFi/app connectivity for select product lines.

We work closely with clients to adapt existing platforms or develop entirely new products. Typical development timeline: 25-35 days for custom mold development, 7-15 days for sample production, and 25-40 days for mass production.
