Quality & Supply Coordination

A transparent process from product brief and supplier matching to sample approval, inspection and shipment

A CLEAR DIVISION OF RESPONSIBILITY

MINSEN is the commercial and project-coordination interface. The selected manufacturing partner is responsible for production, factory inspection records and the product documentation it supplies.

This approach keeps expectations explicit: the correct model, factory, specification, sample, price basis and certification scope should be documented rather than inferred from a catalogue image or model name.

01

Product Brief

Clarify target market, product function, performance target, voltage, quantity, price position and required documentation.

02

Supplier Matching

Compare suitable manufacturing partners and existing platforms. Confirmed specifications are collected in writing before quotation.

03

Sample & Customization

Coordinate sample configuration, test criteria, logo, housing, controls, packaging and approval records.

04

Production Follow-up

Track agreed milestones and coordinate pre-shipment checks, packaging confirmation and production photos where arranged.

05

Documentation Review

Confirm supplier certificates, test documents, labels, manuals and market requirements for the exact model.

06

Shipment Coordination

Align packing completion, shipping marks, booking information and export documents with the agreed trade terms.

WHAT SHOULD BE DOCUMENTED

Before a customer order is placed, the project should identify the manufacturing partner, approved sample, model number, technical specification, packaging artwork, trade terms, inspection arrangement and applicable documentation.

Typical sample and production timing is confirmed per platform and customization level. No lead time or factory capacity is presented as guaranteed until the manufacturing partner confirms it for the project.
