Saudi Air Purifier Voltage and Label Checklist

Published: November 15, 2026 · by MINSEN

A Saudi air purifier project should be checked as a complete product configuration, not as a generic unit with a different plug. Electrical ratings, labels, manuals, packaging and supporting documents need to identify the same model and build.

Short answer: confirm the destination supply, frequency, plug or adapter, filter configuration, Arabic artwork and model-specific evidence before quotation. Treat any existing certificate or test report as a starting question, not automatic approval for a private-label version.

1. Confirm the project supply

Start with the actual installation and sales channel. A product planned for a hotel, retailer, distributor or marketplace may have different documentation and packaging requirements even when the purifier platform is similar.

  • Destination country and city or region
  • Residential, commercial, hospitality or project end use
  • Expected room size and operating hours
  • Required voltage, frequency and plug type
  • Whether the platform uses a direct AC input or an external DC adapter

A dual-voltage listing is not enough on its own. Ask the supplier to confirm the selected components, cord set, plug, adapter and nameplate for the exact configuration being quoted.

2. Make the electrical data consistent

The quotation, specification sheet, sample nameplate, packaging and technical file should all use the same model number and electrical values. Typical checks include:

ItemWhat to confirm
Rated inputVoltage, frequency, power and the matching plug or adapter
Model identifierModel name and configuration match all documents and the sample
NameplateWording, language, importer/manufacturer information and warnings
Change controlAny component or power change is documented before approval

3. Match filtration to the application

For dusty environments, service access matters as much as a headline airflow number. Compare the complete filter sequence, replacement parts and maintenance plan:

  • Pre-filter access and cleaning method
  • HEPA grade and the exact filter construction
  • Activated carbon or other media, if included
  • Availability of replacement filters for the chosen platform
  • Airflow, room coverage and noise targets under the intended operating mode

Examples from the current quotation-based library include COLIN PRO at 600 m³/h with H13 HEPA, Jupiter Plus at 375 m³/h with listed coverage below 45 m², and HALO at 80 m³/h with a pre-filter, HEPA H11 and activated carbon sequence.

4. Plan Arabic artwork and manuals

Label, manual and packaging work should be treated as one project rather than copied from an existing English version. Confirm the channel requirements first, then prepare artwork for review.

  • Product name and model alignment
  • Arabic and English language requirements
  • Safety warnings and installation instructions
  • Replacement-filter information
  • Carton, inner packaging and retail label content
  • Customer and importer approval responsibilities

5. Build the evidence trail

Before relying on a certificate or test report, ask for files that clearly name the proposed product. Check the certificate holder, model number, configuration, standards, validity and destination market. The Saudi importer or conformity provider should confirm the current Product Certificate of Conformity and Shipment Certificate process for the exact project.

Use the official SASO and SABER information as the starting point, then review the exact scope with the importer and conformity provider. Our SASO/SABER readiness guide explains the document questions in more detail.

Pre-quotation checklist

  1. Written destination and channel brief
  2. Confirmed room size, airflow and noise target
  3. Voltage, frequency, plug and adapter specification
  4. Filter configuration and replacement-part plan
  5. Logo, housing and packaging scope
  6. Arabic artwork and manual requirements
  7. Required certificates, reports and model-specific evidence
  8. Estimated annual volume and launch timing

Saudi Air Purifier FAQ

Is changing the plug enough to prepare a Saudi version?

No. Confirm the complete electrical configuration, components, nameplate, documentation and intended use with the manufacturer and conformity provider.

Can I use the same certificate for my private-label model?

Do not assume so. The named model, configuration, certificate holder and destination scope must be checked for the proposed order.

Can packaging be prepared in Arabic?

Arabic manual, label and packaging artwork can be reviewed as part of the project. Final wording and approval depend on the destination, importer and sales channel.
