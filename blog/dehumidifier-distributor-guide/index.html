Dehumidifier Buying Guide for Distributors: Types, Capacity, and OEM Options

Published: May 26, 2026 | by Minsen Technology

Dehumidifiers are one of the fastest-growing home appliance categories globally, driven by climate change, increased awareness of indoor air quality, and expanding construction in humid regions. For distributors looking to add dehumidifiers to their product line, this guide covers everything you need to know — from types and capacity selection to OEM manufacturing and certifications.

Types of Dehumidifiers

Compressor (Refrigerant) Dehumidifiers

These are the most common type. They work by drawing air over cold coils, condensing moisture into a collection tank. Best for warm to moderate climates (above 15°C / 59°F). They are energy-efficient and available in capacities from 10L/day to 80L/day.

Best for: Domestic use, warm climates, basements, general-purpose humidity control

Desiccant Dehumidifiers

These use a moisture-absorbing material (silica gel rotor) to extract water from the air. They work effectively at lower temperatures and are quieter than compressor models. However, they consume more energy and generate some heat during operation.

Best for: Cold climates, low-temperature environments (garages, cabins), ultra-quiet operation

Industrial Dehumidifiers

High-capacity units (50L/day and above) designed for warehouses, factories, storage facilities, and commercial spaces. They often feature continuous drainage, heavy-duty construction, and digital humidity controls.

How to Choose the Right Capacity

Dehumidifier capacity is measured in liters per day (L/day) at standard conditions (30°C, 80% humidity). Here is a practical sizing guide:

Room SizeModerate HumidityHigh Humidity
10-20m²10-12L/day12-15L/day
20-40m²15-20L/day20-30L/day
40-60m²20-30L/day30-50L/day
60-100m²30-50L/day50-80L/day

Key Features to Consider

  • Auto defrost: Essential for cooler climates — prevents ice buildup on coils
  • Continuous drainage: Allows hose connection for 24/7 operation without emptying the tank
  • Humidistat: Built-in humidity sensor with auto mode for hands-free operation
  • Energy Star certification: Important for markets with energy labeling requirements (EU ERP, US Energy Star)
  • Noise level: Budget models can be noisy (45-50dB). Premium quiet models run at 38-42dB
  • Portability: Casters and handles make a big difference in customer satisfaction

Market Demand by Region

Europe: Strong demand for compact 10-20L/day models. Energy efficiency (ERP) is critical. Popular in UK, Germany, Netherlands, Scandinavia for basement and winter humidity control.

Middle East: High-capacity 30-50L/day models preferred. SASO certification required for Saudi Arabia. High demand during summer months.

Southeast Asia: Growing market for both residential and commercial. Price-sensitive. 15-30L/day models with basic features are bestsellers.

North America: Large market for 30-50L/day models with Energy Star certification. Strong seasonal demand pattern (spring-summer peak).

OEM Considerations for Dehumidifiers

Most dehumidifier OEM production in China follows the same process as air purifiers:

  • Certifications: CE, RoHS, SASO, ERP available from experienced manufacturers
  • Packaging: Custom packaging, manuals (including language localization), and warranty cards
