Saudi Air Purifier OEM Project Brief: What to Send

Published: November 15, 2026 · by MINSEN

A useful OEM/ODM conversation starts before the quotation. The buyer should explain what the product must achieve in Saudi Arabia, while the supplier should identify the product platform and information gaps that could affect sampling or production.

Short answer: send the target channel, room size or commercial use, annual volume, voltage and plug, filter preference, noise target, branding and packaging scope, required documents and launch timing. A model preference is useful, but the application should come first.

Start with the commercial use

A distributor, retailer, hotel project and private-label brand may start from different requirements. Write down the channel and what the product needs to do:

  • Destination and end-use application
  • Estimated room size, ceiling height or commercial area
  • Operating hours and occupancy
  • Dust exposure and filter-service expectations
  • Noise target for bedrooms, offices or public spaces
  • Target price position and estimated annual volume

Write the electrical requirement

Specify the electrical requirement as a product configuration, not only as “Saudi version.” Include voltage, frequency, plug type and whether the selected platform uses an external adapter.

Brief itemExample of a useful answer
Voltage / frequency220–240V / 50Hz — confirm with selected model
Plug / adapterDestination plug to be confirmed; direct AC or DC adapter
ControlsManual, touch, remote or connected functions required
DocumentsList the reports or documents the importer or channel expects

Compare actual platforms

The current quotation-based library includes compact, medium-room and larger-airflow purifier platforms. These are examples for comparison, not automatic recommendations for every Saudi project.

  • COLIN PRO: 600 m³/h, H13 HEPA, 60 W and 220 V listed.
  • Jupiter Plus: 375 m³/h, listed coverage below 45 m², 40 W and below 61 dB(A). DC 24 V adapter configuration must be confirmed.
  • HALO: 80 m³/h, pre-filter plus HEPA H11 and activated carbon, 35 W and an AC 220–240 V option listed.

Do not choose only by airflow. Filter construction, replacement parts, service access, noise, packaging and documentation can change the commercial fit.

Define branding and packaging

Provide a practical scope instead of a general request for “customization.” Useful details include:

  • Logo treatment and housing color
  • Control language and button names
  • Manual and label languages
  • Retail box, inner protection and carton marking
  • Required warnings and replacement-filter information
  • Artwork owner and approval responsibility

Tooling, print minimums and packaging changes can affect the quotation. Confirm where existing platform customization ends and a new development begins.

Plan the sample and evidence review

The sample should match the approved specification, labels and accessories. Before sampling, agree on the approval record:

  1. Exact model and configuration
  2. Performance and noise data to be checked
  3. Filter, controls and accessories
  4. Nameplate, manual and packaging artwork
  5. Available test reports and certificates
  6. Required changes and responsible owner
  7. Inspection criteria for production

For Saudi documentation, use the voltage and label checklist and the SASO/SABER readiness guide. The importer and conformity provider should confirm the current process for the exact model.

Project brief template

  1. Destination city or region
  2. Sales channel and buyer type
  3. Room size or commercial application
  4. Preferred model or comparable airflow
  5. Voltage, frequency and plug
  6. Filter and noise requirements
  7. Estimated annual volume
  8. Brand, housing and packaging scope
  9. Required documents
  10. Target sample date and launch date

Saudi OEM Project FAQ

Do I need every answer before contacting a supplier?

No. Start with the application, destination, channel, estimated volume and any model preference. The supplier can identify the missing technical and commercial questions.

Can one quotation cover several Saudi channels?

It can start the discussion, but packaging, documentation and performance requirements may differ by channel. Confirm the exact configuration and approval criteria for each version.

When should certification be discussed?

Before quotation and sampling. The exact model, configuration and destination should be reviewed with the importer and conformity provider so that evidence and responsibilities are clear.
