Air Purifier OEM Cost Breakdown: What Affects the Price in 2026

Published: May 26, 2026 | by Minsen Technology

"How much does it cost to OEM an air purifier?" is the most common question we hear from new clients. The honest answer: it depends on multiple factors. This guide breaks down every cost component so you can budget accurately for your private label air purifier project.

Cost Component 1: Mold Development (One-Time)

If you want a fully custom housing design, you will need a custom mold. This is the biggest upfront investment.

Customization LevelMold Cost (USD)Timeline
Existing design, your logo + color$0 (no new mold)Sample: 7 days
Modified existing design$2,000 - $8,000+10-15 days
Fully custom design$8,000 - $25,00025-35 days

Pro tip: First-time importers should start with an existing factory design with logo and color customization. This eliminates mold costs entirely and significantly reduces lead time. You can invest in custom molds once your sales volume justifies it.

Cost Component 2: Per-Unit Pricing (FOB Shenzhen)

Unit prices vary primarily by CADR rating, filter quality, and features. Here are estimated price ranges for air purifier OEM orders of 500-1000 units:

Cost Component 3: MOQ Impact on Unit Price

Your order quantity directly affects per-unit pricing. Here is how MOQ typically influences price for a mid-range CADR 300 model:

  • 500 units: Baseline price (100%)
  • 1,000 units: 5-10% discount from baseline
  • 3,000 units: 10-15% discount
  • 5,000+ units: 15-25% discount

Most OEM factories offer flexible MOQs — starting from 500 units for first-time partners, with lower repeat MOQs (50-100 units) once you have established trust and a sales track record.

Cost Component 4: Certification Costs

Certifications are required for most target markets and are typically paid separately from unit pricing:

  • CE testing + documentation: $1,000 - $3,000 per model (valid indefinitely with EU Declaration of Conformity)
  • SASO/SABER (Saudi Arabia): $1,500 - $3,000 + $200-500/year for SABER registration
  • RoHS testing: Often included in CE, or $300-500 standalone
  • ERP energy labeling (EU): $500 - $1,000
  • UL (USA): $5,000 - $15,000 (most expensive — but required for US retail)

Important: Some factories already hold certifications for their existing models. If you choose an existing design, you may be able to use the factory's existing certificates (with your brand name added) at a reduced cost.

Cost Component 5: Packaging and Branding

  • Custom color box: $0.50 - $2.00 per unit
  • User manual printing: $0.10 - $0.30 per unit (multi-language costs more)
  • Logo branding (silkscreen, pad print, or label): $0.20 - $0.80 per unit
  • Gift box / premium packaging: $2.00 - $5.00 per unit

Cost Component 6: Shipping and Logistics

FOB Shenzhen (most common): You arrange shipping from the Chinese port. Sea freight costs depend on destination:

  • To Middle East (Jeddah, Dammam): $1,500 - $3,500 per 20ft container
  • To Europe (Hamburg, Rotterdam): $2,000 - $4,000 per 20ft container
  • To USA (Los Angeles, New York): $2,500 - $5,000 per 20ft container
  • To Southeast Asia (Bangkok, Singapore): $500 - $1,500 per 20ft container

A 20ft container holds approximately 800-1,200 air purifiers depending on size and packaging.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): Many factories offer door-to-door DDP service with all costs included. This is ideal for first-time importers who want a single all-in price.
