Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Minsen Technology ("we", "our", "us") operates the website minsenair.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our OEM/ODM manufacturing services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you submit an inquiry via our contact form or WhatsApp, we may collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, and details about your project requirements.

Automatically Collected Data: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymized data about website usage, including pages visited, time spent, and geographic region. This data is anonymized and cannot identify you personally.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your OEM/ODM inquiries and provide quotations
  • To communicate with you about your project or order
  • To improve our website and services based on usage analytics
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Formspree — Our contact form submission processor
  • Google Analytics — Anonymized website usage tracking
  • WhatsApp/Meta — When you contact us via WhatsApp

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact inquiry data for up to 2 years after your last interaction with us, or until you request deletion. Analytics data is retained for 14 months per Google's standard retention policy.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & Other Jurisdictions)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@minsenair.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our website uses the following cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat) — Anonymized traffic analysis

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use our website or submit inquiries.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including SSL/HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted through our website.
