Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from B2B partners.

📦 What is your MOQ? ▼
Standard MOQ starts at 500 units per model for first-time OEM partners. For repeat orders, we offer flexible MOQs as low as 50-100 units. We grow with you.
🎨 Can you customize design and packaging? ▼
Absolutely. Full OEM/ODM: custom housing, logo, color, filter specs, control panel, packaging. Custom mold development: 25-35 days.
⏱ What is the lead time? ▼
Samples: 7-15 days. Mass production: 25-40 days depending on quantity and customization.
✅ What certifications do you have? ▼
CE, RoHS, ERP, ISO 9001:2015. We can also assist with UL, CB, SASO, and other market-specific certifications.
🚢 What are your shipping terms? ▼
FOB Shenzhen or CIF your port. Sea freight 20-35 days. Air freight 5-8 days. DDP service available for select markets.
🔧 What warranty do you offer? ▼
12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranty options available. Spare parts inventory maintained.
