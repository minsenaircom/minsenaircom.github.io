HOW COMPLIANCE IS CONFIRMED

Certification files belong to the selected manufacturing partner and are verified for the exact model, configuration and destination market.

1. Confirm the Target Market

Country, sales channel and required safety or energy documentation are defined before quotation.

2. Verify the Exact Model

The manufacturing partner confirms the applicable certificates, reports, model numbers and validity dates.

3. Review the Documents

Available files are reviewed before the customer commits to an order or uses compliance claims in marketing.

4. Confirm in Writing

The final model, documentation scope and responsibilities are recorded in the quotation or project documents.
