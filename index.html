Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our B2B partners about OEM/ODM cooperation.

📦 What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? ▼
Our standard MOQ starts at 200 pieces per model for first-time OEM partners. For established clients or repeat orders, we offer flexible MOQs as low as 50-100 units depending on the product line. We believe in growing together with our partners.
🎨 Can you customize the product design and packaging? ▼
Absolutely. We offer full OEM/ODM services including custom housing design, logo branding, color matching, filter specifications, control panel layout, and packaging design. You provide the brief — our engineering team handles the rest. Custom mold development typically takes 25-35 days.
⏱ What is the typical lead time for an OEM order? ▼
Sample production: 7-15 days after design confirmation.
Mass production: 25-40 days depending on order quantity and customization level. We provide a detailed production schedule after order confirmation and keep you updated at every milestone.
✅ What certifications do your products have? ▼
Our products are certified with CE (European), RoHS, SASO/SABER (Saudi Arabia), CCC (China), ERP (Energy), and ISO 9001:2015 quality management. We can also assist with UL, CB, and other market-specific certifications as needed for your target market.
🚢 What are your shipping terms and delivery options? ▼
We ship FOB Shenzhen or CIF to your designated port. Shipping worldwide via sea freight (20-35 days) or air freight (5-8 days). For LCL orders, we handle consolidation. We also offer door-to-door DDP service for select markets.
🔧 What warranty do you offer for OEM products? ▼
We provide a 12-month warranty from the date of shipment covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranty options are available upon negotiation. We maintain a spare parts inventory and provide technical support throughout the warranty period.
