A CLEARER WAY TO SOURCE

MINSEN is an independent sourcing brand. I work directly with importers, private-label owners and distributors who need help turning a product requirement into a clear, verifiable manufacturing project.

Manufacturing is completed by qualified partner factories. My role is to compare platforms, organise technical information, coordinate samples, follow production and keep communication clear from inquiry to shipment.

This model is deliberately transparent. You know who is coordinating the project, what has been confirmed, and which manufacturing partner is responsible for production.

QUALITY CONTROL & COMPLIANCE

Specification control — Product parameters are taken from the manufacturing partner's current written specification, not estimated from model names or marketing images.

Compliance coordination — Certificates, reports, labels and declarations are confirmed for the exact model and destination market before they are presented to the end customer.

OUR ADVANTAGE

You get one point of contact for supplier comparison, technical clarification, sample coordination, inspection follow-up, packaging approval and export communication. I can also join factory calls and visits by appointment.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOUR PROJECT

Product and market

Product category, destination country and intended sales channel.

Commercial target

Estimated annual volume, target price range and launch timing.

Technical requirements

Voltage, plug, capacity, performance, language and required certification.

Customization

Logo, housing, controls, packaging or other branding requirements.
