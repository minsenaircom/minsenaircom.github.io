What Must Be Adapted for South America

Power configuration

Brazil commonly requires 127V or 220V at 60Hz depending on the region and product plan. Spanish-speaking markets also differ. The electrical version must be defined per SKU.

Language and artwork

Portuguese for Brazil and Spanish for other target markets should be planned for manuals, controls, labels and packaging rather than added after the product is complete.

Serviceability

Accessible filters, standard service parts and clear maintenance procedures are important for importers that need local after-sales support.

Price-tier design

Keep the range easy to explain: entry, smart mid-range, cool-climate and commercial. Avoid launching with dozens of similar models.

Brazilian and Regional Compliance

Brazil can require product-specific conformity assessment, and connected products may introduce additional telecommunications requirements. For Spanish-speaking countries, requirements depend on the product, importer and destination.

We review the product category, electrical version, wireless functions and destination before confirming certification scope. We do not assume that an existing European document automatically covers a South American market. See our dehumidifier distributor guide for general product-selection considerations.

Sample approval, language, packaging, MOQ, inspection and delivery schedule.

South America OEM Questions

Should Brazil and Spanish-speaking countries use the same model?

They can share a product platform where the technical requirements align, but voltage, language, packaging and compliance must be planned separately.

Can WiFi be removed for a lower-cost version?

For platforms designed with optional connectivity, a non-WiFi configuration can be reviewed. The control board and user interface must be confirmed with the selected platform.

Can you support private-label packaging?

Yes. Logo, color, packaging structure and manual content can be discussed as part of the OEM/ODM scope, subject to artwork approval and minimum project quantities.

Can you supply replacement filters?

Filter supply and service-parts planning can be included in the commercial discussion so the importer can support the installed product base.
