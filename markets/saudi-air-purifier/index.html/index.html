What to Confirm for Saudi Arabia

Electrical configuration

Confirm the destination supply, frequency, plug, adapter and component suitability for the exact model. A dual-voltage listing does not replace project confirmation.

Dust and filter service

Review pre-filter access, filter sequence, replacement parts and realistic maintenance planning for the intended environment.

Arabic artwork

Plan manual, label, control and packaging reviews together. Final wording and approval remain project-specific.

Documentation

Check the model number, configuration, certificate holder, validity and intended market. Do not assume an existing document covers a different model.

Read the Saudi voltage and label checklist, prepare a Saudi OEM project brief, or review the full SASO/SABER readiness guide.

Compare verified specifications, filter design, packaging and model-specific documentation.

3. Approve the sample

Review performance fields, build, labels, controls, accessories and packaging before production.

4. Plan production

Confirm MOQ, lead time, inspection, documents and shipping responsibilities in writing.

Typical project terms: MOQ 500 units per model and configuration; production approximately 45 days after specification and sample approval. Tooling and major design changes may require separate confirmation.

Saudi Air Purifier OEM FAQs

Can you recommend a model for a villa or hotel?

Send the room volume, ceiling height, target airflow, noise limit and operating pattern. COLIN PRO, Jupiter Plus and HALO can serve as comparison points, but suitability must be checked against the exact application.

Can the product be supplied for 220–240V?

A 220–240V and 50Hz configuration can be reviewed for a selected platform. Confirm the plug, adapter and electrical components for the exact destination and model before approval.

Can I use my own logo and packaging?

Logo, housing color, controls and packaging can be reviewed for the selected platform. Feasibility and tooling requirements are confirmed against the model and order quantity.

Do you provide SASO or SABER certification for every unit?

No blanket claim is made. The requirement and available evidence depend on the exact model, configuration, importer and current Saudi process. The applicable path is confirmed with the importer or conformity provider.
