How to Shortlist for North America

Short answer: start with the intended channel and required documentation. A US or Canadian version is not created by changing the plug alone; electrical components, ratings, performance claims and product documentation must be reviewed for the exact model.

Residential Dehumidifiers

Compare 115V / 60Hz platforms using the supplier's current North American specification and applicable capacity test method.

Do not convert L/day ratings into pints/day by simple arithmetic.

Commercial and Restoration Channels

For property management, water-damage restoration and construction drying, confirm duty cycle, drainage, electrical safety and service requirements.

Residential Air Purifiers

For bedrooms and living spaces, compare verified CADR, noise, filter cost and the required safety or air-cleaner documentation.

Large-Room and Smoke Applications

Match airflow and filtration to room volume and the specific claim being made. Ozone-producing technologies require separate evaluation.

Model data notice: ask for the current supplier-backed North American specification before comparing products or requesting samples.

Why a US Version Is Not Only a Plug Change

Capacity testing

Dehumidifier capacity depends on test temperature and humidity. L/day results from one market cannot simply be relabelled as US pints/day without evaluation under the applicable method.

Electrical safety

Components, wiring, power input and enclosures must be reviewed for 115V / 60Hz operation and the safety certification route required by the channel.

Air-cleaner requirements

Air purifiers may require additional review of ozone generation, electrical safety and performance claims. Products using ionizers or plasma should not be approved without checking the exact configuration.

Wireless requirements

WiFi and app-connected models require the wireless module and documentation to be reviewed for US and Canadian requirements.

Applications That Fit the Market

Certification and Performance Review

North American requirements depend on product category, model, components and channel. A safety listing is commonly requested by retailers, while dehumidifier performance and air-cleaner requirements can introduce additional testing and labelling considerations.

We confirm the applicable scope before quotation and do not use “UL compliant”, “CARB approved” or similar wording unless the exact model and supporting documentation justify it. Our air purifier certification guide explains why market requirements must be checked separately.

North America OEM Questions

Is Canada the same as the United States?

They share similar electrical fundamentals, but certification, bilingual packaging and retailer expectations should be reviewed separately.

Should Mexico use the same program as the US?

No. Mexico should be planned as a separate commercial and technical program, including voltage, language, documentation and channel requirements.

Can obsolete or ozone-generating products be adapted for the US?

Each configuration must be reviewed against current requirements. We avoid treating an existing ozone-generating product as automatically suitable for North America.

Can the brand control the product design?

OEM/ODM scope can include housing appearance, logo, controls, packaging, filters and selected specifications, subject to tooling, MOQ and validation.
