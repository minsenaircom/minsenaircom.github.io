WHAT I CHECK BEFORE PRESENTING A PRODUCT

Compliance is not inherited from a catalogue or assumed from a CE logo. The exact model, supplier and destination market must be identified.

1. Model Match

Check that the document identifies the correct model, electrical configuration and supplier.

2. Market Scope

Confirm the applicable safety, energy, wireless or labelling requirements for the destination country.

3. Validity

Review issue dates, standards, certificate holder and coverage for the version being quoted.

4. Customer Approval

Share only approved documents after the supplier and customer agree on what may be disclosed.
